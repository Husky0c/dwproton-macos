# Third-party components

The port is based on Wine and Dawn Winery's Wine fork. The Wine project contributors are listed in the upstream `AUTHORS` file. Wine and the Darwin patch are licensed under the LGPL 2.1 or later. The shared-surface adaptation draws from CodeWeavers' published CrossOver 26.3.0 Wine source. [Wine](https://www.winehq.org/) · [Dawn Winery Wine](https://dawn.wine/dawn-winery/wine-dwproton) · [CodeWeavers source downloads](https://www.codeweavers.com/crossover/source)

The binary distribution contains these runtime libraries. The source archive contains the corresponding source releases and their original licensing notices.
